Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectify (Art. 16),
and erase (Art. 17) personal data concerning you, the right to restrict
processing (Art. 18), the right to data portability (Art. 20), and the
right to object (Art. 21).

Practical note: this application stores all data in a local project
folder on the machine it runs on. Deleting that folder permanently
removes all processed data. Exports you have already sent to the
research team can be deleted on request via the controller contact
below — exports are identified by institution and date, not by person.
